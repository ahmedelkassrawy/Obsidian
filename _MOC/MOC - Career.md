# MOC - Career

This is the job-hunting half of the vault: interview prep, the CV and application material, and the plans for what to learn next. It is strongest on interview prep - the Orange MLOps folder is a complete, well-organised answer set with its own one-page visual index, and the two InstaBug notes cover real written-test questions on indexes, concurrency and HTTP.
It is weakest on everything before the interview. There is no CV, no portfolio or project write-up, no salary or offer notes, and no record of which companies were applied to or how each one went. The ALX notes are prompt dumps rather than lessons, and PLAN.md is eight bare links with nothing written about any of them.
The 'Knowledge Gaps Audit 2026-09-15' note is the companion to this map: it lists, folder by folder, which technical topics are missing - and those gaps are exactly the ones an interviewer will find.
